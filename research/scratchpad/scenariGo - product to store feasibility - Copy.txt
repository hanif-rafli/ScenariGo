structure of the network flow:
source -> warehouse-product pair layer ->store-product layer -> sink

- warehouse-product pair layer: a pair represents the product stored in a certain warehouse.
-store-product layer: a pair represents the product that a certain store requested.   

connection and capacity between:
- FROM source TO warehouse-product: there will be a connection from the source to each one. the capacity in the edge is the amount of products stored in that warehouse

[BFS/DFS is used in the following section, refer to "[TRAVERSAL SECTION]" below]
-FROM warehouse-product TO store-product: given a warehouse-product pair, there will be a "land connection" made to a store-product pair IF the store is reachable by land and the product matches. There will be a "shipment connection" to a store-product pair if the product matches but is not land-reachable ie. it comes from another "island". the capacity for each connection made is the amount of that product stored in that warehouse.

important note: there must be a distinction between land-reachable and air/water shipment connections.   

- FROM store-product TO sink: there is a connection made from each pair to the sink. the capacity on the edge is the the amount requestewarehouse-productd by that store for that product.
